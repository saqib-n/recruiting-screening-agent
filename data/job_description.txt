Job Title: Backend Software Engineer (Mid-Level)

We're looking for a mid-level backend engineer to join our small product team.

Must have:
- 3+ years professional experience building backend services
- Strong Python experience, comfortable working in a production codebase
- Experience with REST APIs and relational databases (Postgres or similar)
- Comfortable working independently with minimal oversight

Nice to have:
- Experience with cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Experience with a modern async framework (FastAPI, Django, etc.)
- Any experience mentoring or reviewing other engineers' code

Not required:
- A specific degree or university
- Prior experience at a "big name" company

We care about what you can actually build and how you communicate about tradeoffs, not where you went to school or who you've worked for.
